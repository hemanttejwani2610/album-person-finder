Scores compare face embeddings. They are similarity scores, not probabilities. A lower threshold shows more candidates, including more wrong ones.

About accuracy

Matching is approximate. Lighting, head pose, blur, occlusion (sunglasses, masks, hands), small or distant faces, low-resolution thumbnails and age differences between photos all reduce accuracy. Expect both missed photos and false matches, and review results before relying on them.

Diagnostics

These checks only read the page and fetch a few of the album's own thumbnails. Reports contain counts, sizes and masked URL shapes, never images or full links.
